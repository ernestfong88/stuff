# Resident photos

Drop resident portraits here as `<residentId>.jpg` (for example `r1.jpg` for
Marty Martin). They are used on the Residents page, table cards, the
"Do you know the Residents?" game and the shift review.

Residents without a photo show their initials on their avatar colour, so the
app works with this folder empty. Photos are optional and never required.

The ids are in `src/data/seed/residents.json`.
