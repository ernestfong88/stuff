# Resident photos

Add resident portraits here named by resident id, for example `r1.jpg` for
Marty Martin (ids are in `src/data/seed/residents.json`). `.jpg`, `.png` and
`.webp` all work. They are picked up at build time and shown on the Residents
page, table cards, the shift review and the "Do you know the Residents?" game.

Residents without a photo show their initials on their avatar colour, so this
folder can stay empty.
