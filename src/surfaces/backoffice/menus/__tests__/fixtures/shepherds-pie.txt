SHEPHERD'S PIE

A Sunday favourite from the Sequoia kitchen. Rich lamb and vegetables under a golden mashed potato crust.

Serves 8 | Prep 25 min | Cook: 1 hr 10 min

Ingredients
- 2 lb ground lamb
- 1 large onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 1/2 cups beef stock
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- ½ cup frozen peas
- 3 lb russet potatoes, peeled
- 4 tbsp butter
- 1/2 cup whole milk
- Salt and pepper, to taste

Method
1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
2. Brown the lamb in a large pan, then add the onion, carrot and garlic and cook
   until soft, about 8 minutes.
3. Stir in the tomato paste, stock and Worcestershire sauce. Simmer 15 minutes, then add the peas.
4. Boil the potatoes until tender. Mash with the butter and milk.
5. Spread the filling in a 9x13 pan, top with the mash and bake 30 minutes until golden.

Plating
- Spoon a square portion onto a warm plate, crust side up.

Garnish: chopped parsley

Equipment: 9x13 pan, large saute pan, potato masher

Notes
Holds hot for 45 minutes. For Mechanical Altered, serve with extra gravy.
